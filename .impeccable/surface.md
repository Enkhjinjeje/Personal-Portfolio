# Homepage
Mode: Experience. Audience: prospective collaborators and recruiters (assumption).

## Direction contract
THESIS: A personal introduction that connects software and community. The work is about bringing people together, represented by an interactive typographic signature.
OWN-WORLD: White paper, pure black, generous open space, Manrope typography, and a single oversized name. Neutral grays support legibility. No ornamental badges or invented project imagery.
STORY: Meet Jeje, explore the cultural nonprofit project, see technical and leadership experience, and get in touch.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Small name/navigation, a left-aligned introduction and right-aligned profile note, then an oversized interactive Jeje wordmark occupying the full width. Featured work begins immediately below.
FORM: User-pinned monochrome contemporary portfolio inspired by jithinrp.com; seed:6dabe8c7c95bdfaf. A focused code-led pass follows the user's explicit token-budget constraint, replacing expansive comp generation and direction rounds.
FINISH: Browser-verified desktop/mobile behavior, real résumé content, design documentation, and a focused review. No shipping raster other than verified source imagery.
